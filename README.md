### 🌟 About Me

I am a 17-year-old software developer, AI engineer, and tech entrepreneur from **Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan 🇰🇬** with hands-on engineering experience across full-stack applications, computer vision, and AI-driven automation.

- 🔭 **What I build:** Production Computer Vision pipelines, OCR automation, educational evaluation platforms, data analytics solutions, and scalable software.
- 🤖 **AI-Native Engineering:** I integrate cutting-edge agentic workflows (**Antigravity**, **Claude Code**, **Codex**) into my daily development routine to architect, build, and debug complex systems with maximum speed and precision.
- 💼 **Entrepreneurship:** Co-founder at **Cosmic Team** (Restaurant POS software) and core developer on **AIM EDU** (high-throughput exam checking platform).
- 🎯 **Mission:** Pursuing higher education in computer science while building tech infrastructure that makes a tangible impact in Kyrgyzstan and beyond.
